## What the Claude connection can and can't do

- **Can:** see your workspace, credit balance and plan, browse the model catalogue, and read back anything you have already generated (with download links).
- **Can't:** generate images or video, upload our creatives, or buy credits. Generation has to be started by you in the Higgsfield app.

So the workflow is: you run the prompts below in Higgsfield, tell me in the thread, and I pull the results through the connection and build them into the creatives and Reel covers.

## What AI should and shouldn't touch

The 46 creatives are already finished graphic designs with exact text, the real moon mark and the brand palette. Image models redraw logos and garble text, so **do not run the PNGs through Higgsfield to "improve" them**. Use Higgsfield for what we are missing: moving footage and atmosphere for the Reels.

House rules for every generation:

- No logos, wordmarks or text in the prompt. The real moon mark and all copy go on top in the edit.
- No people presented as Moon Padel players, and no crowds presented as a real Moon Padel night. Real faces come from the first night.
- No venue that could be mistaken for a confirmed venue. These are mood shots.
- Check the credit cost Higgsfield shows before you press generate. 100 credits will not cover everything, so run in the order below and use draft or 480p modes first where offered.

## 1. Reel 1 "Something is rising" (top priority)

The hardest Reel to shoot on a phone, and the one AI does best.

- **Model:** Seedance 2.5 (Higgsfield's default video model)
- **Settings:** text to video, 9:16, 8 seconds, draft on first try, then 1080p for the keeper. Audio off (we add the bounce and bass hit in the edit).
- **Prompt:**

> Extreme macro shot of a single padel ball in total darkness. A narrow beam of cold blue-white light from one floodlight catches only the edge of the ball, so it reads as a thin crescent moon. The ball rotates very slowly and the lit crescent grows wider, revealing felt texture and the curved seam. Background pure black with deep navy falloff (#05070F), faint electric blue rim light (#1E9BFF). Locked-off camera, very slow push in. Cinematic, crushed blacks, fine film grain, no text, no logos, no people.

- **Edit:** cut from the last frame to P01 so the ball's crescent lines up with the moon mark.

## 2. Reel 2 hook and B-roll: ball on glass

Short inserts that make the brand film feel premium before we have event footage.

- **Model:** Seedance 2.5, 9:16, 5 seconds, audio off
- **Prompt A (glass hit):**

> Slow motion close-up of a padel ball hitting the glass back wall of an indoor padel court at night. Tiny dust particles in a blue floodlight beam. Dark navy surroundings, electric blue highlights (#1E9BFF), reflections on the glass. Camera static at ball height. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no people.

- **Prompt B (floodlights on):**

> Wide shot of an empty outdoor padel court at night in a modern city, completely dark. Floodlights switch on one bank at a time with a soft flicker, flooding the blue court with cold white light. Warm city haze on the horizon, deep navy sky. Camera slowly rises from court level. Cinematic, no text, no logos, no people, no brand signage.

## 4. Reel 4 "Moonrise" transitions (only if credits remain)

Better done in a normal editor (CapCut, Premiere) with zooms between P01, P09 and P12, because AI morphs distort the moon mark. If you want to try anyway: Seedance 2.5, omni reference mode, start image P09-story-countdown-7.png, end image P09-story-countdown-3.png, 4 seconds, prompt "smooth slow zoom into the glowing moon, seamless transition, keep the graphic unchanged". Expect to reject results where the mark changes shape.
