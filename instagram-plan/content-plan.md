# Moon Padel Dubai: Instagram Content Plan

Pre-launch and post-launch, with every creative designed. Prepared 28 November 2026 for the founders. Everything here is a proposal until you approve it.

**One goal:** more people at Moon Padel nights. That means more players booked onto the 16 spots, more new faces in the D League, more friends and spectators at the venue for the podium reveal. Every post below has a job in that chain: **see it, message MOON on WhatsApp, register, turn up, come back.**

Orange `[BRACKETS]` on the creatives and in the captions are placeholders. Fill them only with confirmed facts: dates, times, venue, fee, WhatsApp number, real spot counts, real names (with the player's OK).

---

## 1. The idea in one page

### What we are selling
Not "a padel tournament". A night out where every point you win is saved forever. The Website Brief already holds the strongest marketing hooks in Dubai padel; this plan puts them on Instagram:

| Hook from the product | Why it brings people in | Where it shows up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Every point saved forever** | Nobody else in Dubai social padel keeps your points. Gives a reason to start now and to come back. | Teaser 2, How it works 8/8, Ad A, medal posts |
| **10-medal journey, Earth to Moon** | A collectible. People come back for the next medal, and share it when they get one. | Medal carousel, medal unlock Story, monthly posts |
| **Secret round 6 and the podium reveal** | Built-in drama. Great Reels, and a reason for friends to come and watch. | Teaser 4, Scores locked Story, Reveal Reel, Podium post |
| **Your name on the big screen** | Ego and fun. Seeing yourself live is the moment people film. | Ad C, live round Stories |
| **D League for beginners** | Removes the biggest barrier: "I'm not good enough for a tournament". | First tournament? post, Ad B, Find your league |
| **New partner every round** | No partner needed. Solo players can just come. | How it works, Bring your crew |

### The campaign line (concept, not approved)
**"Every point counts."** Short, true to the product, works in captions, on posters and on the big screen. The founders' brand line **"Play beyond."** stays on the logo lockup. The brief's own phrase "Every point you win is saved forever" is used as the explainer line.

### The pre-launch device: Moonrise
The countdown is a moon filling up. Crescent on the first teaser, quarter mid-way, gibbous the day before, full moon on launch night. It's ours (the moon mark is the logo), it needs no explanation, and it tells followers how close launch is without a single word. We use "Moonrise" for the countdown and avoid rockets and launch-pad imagery, which would point people to Rocket Padel.

### Five footfall levers this plan pulls
1. **Lower the barrier.** Beginner-first messaging (D League, no partner needed, your name is already on the tablet).
2. **Make it social.** Bring-your-crew posts, tag-a-friend captions, spectators invited to the reveal.
3. **Make it collectable.** Lifetime points and 10 medals mean one night leads to the next.
4. **Make it visible.** Live Stories, podium posts, medal share cards, so every player advertises the next night to their own followers.
5. **Make it easy.** One action everywhere: WhatsApp "MOON". Same word on every creative, bio link, Story link sticker and ad.

## 2. The theme, refined from the Website Brief

The creatives use the brief's deep-space look (galaxy backgrounds, star fields, glowing blue edges, glass cards, Montserrat headlines, big glowing numbers) and the founders' logo files exactly as supplied. What I tightened for Instagram:

- **One palette, from the brief.** Deep Space `#05070F`, Midnight Navy `#0B1226`, Electric Blue `#1E9BFF`, Glow Blue `#3FB8FF`, Moon Silver `#DDE6F2`. Court blue `#1D3FA6` and Ice `#A9DBFF` from the logo kit are kept for depth and labels. Gold `#F2C14E`, silver `#C9D3E0` and bronze `#D08A55` appear only for podium places and the Moon Legend medal. Signal Orange is used only for placeholders on drafts, so nothing unconfirmed slips into a post.
- **C League gets its own accent** (soft violet `#9A88FF`, as on the brief's level pills) so D and C posters are told apart at a glance.
- **Moon phases from the real mark.** The mark is masked, never redrawn, with a faint "earthshine" of the dark side so the logo is still recognisable as a crescent.
- **Glow is rationed**, as the brief asks: buttons, numbers, medals and the key word of each headline. Everything else stays calm.
- **Safe zones.** Stories keep text out of the top 250 px and bottom 340 px (Instagram UI). Reel covers keep the title inside the middle 3:4 so the grid crop still reads.
- **Generated backgrounds.** Four seeded galaxy backgrounds (two feed, two Story) so the grid looks varied but consistent. Court photos are still skipped for now, as agreed. When the first night is shot, swap a photo into the player feature template and the Reel covers.

## 7. Reels (scripted)

Every Reel: vertical 1080 x 1920, sound on, subtitles burned in, first frame works without sound, the logo appears only at the end or on the cover. Captions are short; Reel text carries the story.

### Reel 1. "Something is rising" (pre-launch teaser, L-14)
- **Concept:** A padel ball under a single floodlight, lit so only a crescent of it shows, slowly turning until it reads as our moon.
- **Hook (0 to 1 s):** Total darkness, the sound of one ball bounce. On-screen: "Dubai."
- **Shots:** 1) Black, bounce sound. 2) Macro: ball rolling into a sliver of light, crescent edge. 3) Slow rotation, more of the ball lit. 4) Cut to the crescent moon mark (P01 artwork) matching the same curve. 5) Hold on P01.
- **On-screen text:** "Dubai." / "Something is rising." / "Soon."
- **Editing:** Slow, 8 to 10 s total. Bass hit on the cut to the moon. Blue grade, crushed blacks.
- **Caption:** Something is rising over Dubai. Turn on notifications.
- **CTA:** Follow.

### Reel 2. Brand film "Every point counts" (reveal, L-6)
- **Concept:** A night at Moon Padel in 25 seconds, from empty court to podium. If no event has happened yet, shoot a staged night with 16 friends; label nothing as a real event result.
- **Hook:** Close-up of a hand tapping a name on the check-in tablet: "Checked in." On-screen: "Your padel night, but every point counts."
- **Shots:** 1) Tablet tap. 2) Floodlights on, wide court. 3) Four fast rally clips, each ending on the point. 4) Big screen numbers ticking up. 5) Partners swapping, high fives. 6) Screen goes dark: "Scores locked". 7) Gold medal lifted. 8) Moon mark + "WhatsApp MOON".
- **On-screen text:** "16 players." "4 courts." "6 rounds." "New partner every round." "Every point saved." "Round 6 is secret." "Then this." "Moon Padel. Dubai."
- **Editing:** Cut on ball contact. Music with a clear drop at the medal. 25 to 30 s.
- **Caption:** Moon Padel is here. Americano nights in Dubai with live scores, a podium every night and points that stay with you forever. WhatsApp "MOON" to join the first night.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp MOON. Collab with the venue and the players who appear.

### Reel 3. "What is Americano?" (beginner explainer, L-4, then monthly)
- **Concept:** Talking head at the court, one founder, 30 seconds, explaining it to a friend who's never played one. Cover: L19.
- **Hook:** "You don't need a partner to play a padel tournament. Here's how."
- **Shots:** Founder to camera, cut-aways to the court diagram (How it works slide 5) and the 6-3 scoring slide.
- **On-screen text:** "No partner? Fine." "New partner every round." "Win, move up a court." "Every point you win is yours." "Beginners play in the D League."
- **Editing:** Jump cuts, subtitles word by word, graphics popping in from the carousel.
- **Caption:** Never played an Americano? It's the easiest way into competitive padel. Save this for later, or send it to the friend who keeps saying they're "not good enough yet".
- **CTA:** Send to a friend. WhatsApp MOON.

### Reel 4. "Moonrise" (launch day morning)
- **Concept:** The countdown stories as one sequence: crescent, quarter, gibbous, full, then the first night poster.
- **Hook:** "14 days ago we posted this." (P01 on screen)
- **Shots:** P01, P09 7, P09 3, P09 1, P09 Tonight, P12. Each morphs into the next.
- **On-screen text:** "14 days." "7." "3." "1." "Tonight."
- **Editing:** Smooth zooms into the moon, beat-synced. 12 s.
- **Caption:** Full moon. First night. See you on court.
- **CTA:** Come and watch from [TIME].

### Reel 5. "The reveal" (after every event)
- **Concept:** The podium reveal, filmed live, in one take with reactions. Our signature Reel; every week it's different people.
- **Hook:** Big screen: "Scores locked". On-screen: "Nobody knows who won. Not even them."
- **Shots:** 1) Locked screen. 2) Faces watching. 3) Bronze reveal, reaction. 4) Silver, reaction. 5) Gold, the loudest reaction. 6) Medal round the neck. 7) Group shot.
- **On-screen text:** "Bronze." "Silver." "Gold." "[N] points." "Next night: [DATE]."
- **Editing:** Keep natural sound of the crowd, add a soft beat under it. Slow-mo on the gold moment.
- **Caption:** Round 6 is played in secret. Then this happens. Congratulations [@GOLD], [@SILVER] and [@BRONZE]. Next night [DATE], WhatsApp "MOON".
- **CTA:** Tag the winners. WhatsApp MOON.

### Reel 6. "Winners up, losers down" (match footage)
- **Concept:** Follow one player across six rounds as they climb from court 4 to court 1 (or fall and fight back). Real names with permission.
- **Hook:** "Started on court 4. Watch where they end up."
- **Shots:** Court number sign or TV line-up at each round, one rally from each, final standing.
- **On-screen text:** "Round 1: Court 4." through "Round 6: Court 1."
- **Editing:** Counter in the corner, quick cuts, final freeze on the standings.
- **Caption:** Six rounds, six partners, one climb. That's a Moon Padel night.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp MOON.

### Reel 7. "First medal" (player story)
- **Concept:** A player unlocking First Light (50 points), then receiving the real medal at the venue.
- **Hook:** Phone screen: progress bar "3 pts to go".
- **Shots:** Phone progress bar, last rally, big screen celebration "[NAME] unlocked FIRST LIGHT", medal handed over, player's reaction to camera.
- **On-screen text:** "3 points to go." "Medal 1 of 10." "Next stop: the Moon."
- **Caption:** Every point you win counts towards your next medal. Even the ones from a loss. Welcome to the journey, [@PLAYER].
- **CTA:** WhatsApp MOON to start yours.

### Reel 8. "First tournament nerves" (beginner reassurance)
- **Concept:** Three D League players answer "How nervous were you before your first night?" Honest, funny, short.
- **Hook:** "Be honest. Were you nervous?"
- **Shots:** Three quick interviews court-side, cut with them laughing on court later.
- **On-screen text:** Their answers as subtitles, then "D League: built for beginners."
- **Caption:** Everyone's first night is a first night. That's what the D League is for.
- **CTA:** WhatsApp MOON.

### Reel 9. "POV: you came to watch" (spectator footfall)
- **Concept:** Shot from a friend's point of view: arrive, grab a drink if the venue allows, watch the big screen, scream at the reveal, sign up for next time.
- **Hook:** "POV: your friend dragged you to watch padel."
- **Shots:** Arriving, the floodlit courts, big screen, the reveal, the friend saying "I'm playing next time".
- **Caption:** Come for the reveal, stay for the next night. Spectators always welcome at [VENUE].
- **CTA:** Bring a friend.

### Reel 10. "Team night" (corporate)
- **Concept:** An office team's private night, from suits to sportswear. Only once a real team night has happened.
- **Hook:** "Our team skipped the team dinner."
- **Shots:** Office leaving, arriving, the big screen with company names, a reveal, team photo.
- **Caption:** A team night your office will talk about all week. Groups of [SIZE], venue and date on request. WhatsApp "TEAM".
- **CTA:** WhatsApp TEAM.

## 10. Footfall boosters beyond the feed (concepts for the founders)

None of these are promised in any creative. Approve the ones you want and I'll design them.

1. **Spectators welcome.** Every match-day Story invites people to watch the reveal. If the venue agrees, a simple "Watch free" line on the poster.
2. **Bring-a-first-timer.** A player who brings someone new gets `[PERK]` (for example a free drink or bonus points are both ideas, not decisions). Track it on WhatsApp.
3. **Medal wall at the venue.** A printed board of the 10 medals with a phone stand: the most photographed spot of the night.
4. **Player share kit.** After every event each player gets their medal card (L08) and the podium post by WhatsApp. Every share puts Moon Padel in front of that player's friends.
5. **Venue Collabs.** Post the event poster and the reveal Reel as Collab posts with the venue so they land in the venue's followers' feeds too.
6. **Coach partnerships.** Local coaches send students who are ready for their first Americano to the D League. Content: "Coach's pick" player features.
7. **Monthly flagship (Full Moon Night).** Bigger production on the night nearest the full moon: more lights, music, photographer. Content peak of the month.
8. **Team nights.** Corporate private nights fill quiet slots and bring 8 to 16 new players who never followed padel accounts.

**Animated Reels** (`creatives/motion/mp4/`, 1080 x 1920, silent so you can add a trending track in Instagram)

| File | Length | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| R01-something-rising | 9 s | Teaser 1 as a Reel: the crescent rises and lights up (Reel 1) |
| R02-moonrise-countdown | 13 s | 7, 3, 1, Tonight while the moon fills to full (Reel 4) |
| R03-points-gone | 9 s | Points counter climbs to 347, glitches back to 0000, "Gone." (Teaser 2) |
| R04-podium-reveal | 15 s | Scores locked, then bronze, silver, gold rise one by one; fill names and points after each night |
| R05-medal-journey | 12 s | The 10 medals light up from Earth to the Moon (medal carousel as a Reel) |

Edit the timing or text in `creatives/tools/motion.py` and re-run it (needs ffmpeg).
