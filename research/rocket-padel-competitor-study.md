# Rocket Padel competitor study

15 Nov 2026. Living version: [Claude Doc](https://claude.ai/artifact/4Df85AgTRjA22oEan3XmtY)

## Summary

Rocket Padel is a high-frequency, rating-driven amateur league, not an events brand. It runs about 11 ladder tournaments a week at 170 AED a player across 4 to 5 Dubai clubs, and its product is the system: divisions, a live rating, a monthly race and an app.

The positioning in our launch plan, "Rocket sells the league, Moon Padel sells the night", still holds and is stronger than when it was written. The league space is getting crowded (Padel Power League now advertises 20+ clubs and an AED 25,000+ prize pool), and nobody we found is selling the experience.

The main lesson: copy Rocket's backbone (fair levels, WhatsApp entry, results that bring people back), but do not try to beat it on frequency or system depth. Win on atmosphere, content and players being seen.

One gap in this study: we could not confirm Rocket Padel Dubai's Instagram handle. The site links to no social accounts, and @rocketpadel belongs to an unrelated UK club chain. The social read below relies on the three Rocket ads already in our launch plan.

**Our inference, not sourced:** a full 20-player event grosses about 3,400 AED before court hire, the processing fee and VAT. Court hire is the main cost, so margin depends on the club deal, which is not public. The no-refund, credit-only policy protects cash flow and is worth copying.

The tournament counter rose by 23 in about 16 days, which fits the 10 to 11 games a week on the schedule. Player count rose by 72 in the same window.

## Formats, levels and categories

Every Rocket event is the same product: a 90 or 120 minute ladder tournament for one division. There are no open, women's, beginner, junior or corporate categories on the site ([Rules](https://rocketpadel.ae/rules), [Schedule](https://rocketpadel.ae/schedule)).

- **Divisions:** D4 to D1, D1 strongest. You only play your own division; some players hold two neighbouring divisions while moving.
- **Ladder:** 6 to 8 short rounds, four players per court. Win and you move up a court, lose and you move down. Round one is seeded by rating.
- **Two pair types:** non-fixed (partner changes every round, so you never keep the partner you just played with) and fixed pairs (sign up as a pair, with its own pair rating).
- **Rating:** one number per player from every match, weighted to recent results, recalculated after each tournament. It appears after 5 tournaments and 25 matches. The rules page says 0 to 7, the homepage says 0 to 5, so the site contradicts itself.
- **Points:** Win Points decide medals; court-weighted Game Points break ties and feed the race. Since October 2026, top-court results count 1.5 times bottom-court results.
- **Monthly race:** your best 3 tournaments of the month count, standings reset monthly; all-time standings use your best 10. "Playing more can never hurt."
- **Fair play:** results are entered on court and are final. Sandbagging is called out as "the one thing the ladder can't forgive".

**Our read:** the best-3 rule and the court multiplier are smart. They reward playing often without punishing a bad night, which drives repeat bookings.

## Registration, website and app

Entry is a two-step funnel: message on WhatsApp, play a first tournament, then the league places you in a division. After that, booking and paying happen on the site or app ([Home](https://rocketpadel.ae/), [Terms](https://rocketpadel.ae/terms)).

1. WhatsApp the league (the only contact on the homepage).
2. Get an account; Google sign-in is supported ([Privacy](https://rocketpadel.ae/privacy)). The iOS app says membership is by invitation from the organisers ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6825674989)).
3. Pick a game in your division on the schedule. Pressing Register & pay holds the spot for 15 minutes.
4. Pay through Ziina. If full, join the free waitlist; when a spot opens, the first in line gets 15 minutes to take it.
5. Get a reminder before the deadline, play, and see your rating and standings update.

**Schedule for 15 to 21 Nov 2026:** 11 games, all 170 AED, at Central Padel, All Sports Arena, WPA and Padel Kingdom. Most start at 8:50 PM on weekdays, with 4:20 PM and 5:20 to 7:30 PM weekend slots. Capacity is 16 to 28 players. When checked, the same-day Sunday game showed 11/20 and Thursday showed 3/24; the rest showed 0, several labelled "confirmed" ([Schedule](https://rocketpadel.ae/schedule)).

**Our read on the site:** it is a clean, useful tool (schedule, leaderboards, profiles, rules) but it sells nothing. No photos, no video, no social links, no story about why to play. The clubs page lists Central Padel, Dock & Court, Padel Kingdom and WPA with map links only, while the homepage claims 6 clubs and the schedule uses All Sports Arena, which is not on the clubs page. Low counts could mean late booking or soft demand; we cannot tell from one snapshot.

## Venues, partners and sponsors

Rocket rents courts at existing clubs and shows no branded partnerships. Venues named on the site: Central Padel, Dock & Court, Padel Kingdom, WPA and All Sports Arena, plus an unlinked "Padel Pro" label ([Clubs](https://rocketpadel.ae/clubs), [Schedule](https://rocketpadel.ae/schedule)). No sponsor logos, apparel partner, prize partner or media partner appear anywhere.

**Our read:** Rocket treats clubs as suppliers, not co-promoters. That leaves room for Moon Padel to offer a venue something Rocket does not: a filmed, branded night that the venue can repost and that brings new faces to its courts. Sponsors are an open field in this niche; Padel Power League is already leading with prize money ([padelpowerleague.app](https://www.padelpowerleague.app/)).

## Community mechanics

Rocket's community is built from data, and it works. Every mechanic gives a player a reason to come back next week ([Home](https://rocketpadel.ae/), [Leaderboard](https://rocketpadel.ae/boards)).

- **Leaderboards** by month and all time, split by non-fixed and fixed pairs; November 2026 lists 121 ranked players.
- **Monthly winners:** best player and best pair per division, plus promotion notices.
- **Latest champions** per division, with dates.
- **"On the rise":** biggest rating gains in the past 7 days.
- **Player profiles** with rating, wins, points, optional photo, country flag and playing side.
- **Medals** decided by Win Points.

**What is missing (our read):** faces and stories. Winners are names in a table. There is no visible player spotlight, rivalry storyline, photo gallery or social night around the games. Fixed-pair partner invites exist in the app, but finding a partner or a community group is not shown.

## Instagram, social and brand identity

We could not verify Rocket Padel Dubai's Instagram account, follower count or posting cadence. The site and privacy policy mention no social accounts, Instagram blocks automated reading, and search results for @rocketpadel point to Rocket Padel UK, a separate London club chain with clubs in the UK and Denmark ([rocketpadel.com](https://www.rocketpadel.com/), [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/rocketpadel)).

What we do know comes from the three Rocket ads in our launch plan, shot at Central Padel Dubai:

| Ad | Hook | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We are Dubai's first amateur padel league" (37s) | Claim, then how divisions and ratings work | Fast cuts, white condensed captions, iPad and medals as props |
| "Stop playing social games" (32s) | Problem: beginners in your game, pizza nights | Moody blue, bold green kinetic type, dark grade |
| "My only free evening" POV (53s) | Traffic, paid court, wasted game; league is the fix | UGC style, WhatsApp notification hook |

- **Tone:** direct, competitive, a little confrontational toward casual padel ("battles here are real").
- **Content hero:** the system (divisions, ratings, iPad), not the players or the night.
- **Visual identity:** the site uses a dark green theme colour (#0F1E1A) with no photography; the ads use green and white type on bright courts. Consistent, functional, not premium.
- **Name risk:** sharing a name with a large UK brand that has an EA7 Emporio Armani apparel deal ([news](https://www.rocketpadel.com/news/rocket-padel-announces-ea-7-as-official-apparel-partner)) muddies Rocket Dubai's search and social presence. Moon Padel should lock a clean, consistent handle everywhere before launch.

## Strengths and gaps

Rocket is strong where a league must be strong and weak everywhere a brand would be. Items marked "our read" are judgement, not fact.

| Rocket strength | Why it matters | Gap Moon Padel can use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fair, rating-based levels | Players trust the games | Table stakes; Moon needs it too but should not lead with it |
| About 11 games a week, 4 to 5 clubs | Habit and convenience | Rocket owns frequency; Moon should run fewer, bigger nights |
| WhatsApp entry, online pay, waitlist | Low friction, protected cash flow | Copy it |
| Monthly race, medals, "on the rise" | Reasons to return | Add faces, clips and stories to the same idea |
| App and live standings | Feels serious and organised | Moon can start with a simple results post and sheet |
| No visible sponsors or venue co-marketing (our read) | Revenue relies on fees | Sponsor-ready nights, venue collabs, corporate events |
| No photos, video or story on the site (our read) | Nothing to share or feel | Every player leaves with content |
| One format only, no women's, beginner or social category (our read) | Narrow audience | Category or night formats Rocket does not serve |
| Site inconsistencies (rating scale, club count) | Small trust cost | Get details right and consistent |

## Does our positioning still hold?

Yes. Keep "Rocket sells the league, Moon Padel sells the night", with two refinements.

- **Confirmed:** everything new we found (an invite-only app, a court multiplier, a separate pair rating, a second league leading with prize money) deepens the system and prize race. None of it touches atmosphere, content or the night itself.
- **Refinement 1, do not look casual.** Rocket's ads attack "social games" and "pizza nights". If Moon Padel reads as a party, Rocket's message lands against us. The night must be visibly competitive: levels, live scores, results posted the same night.
- **Refinement 2, pick a rhythm on purpose.** "Fewer, bigger nights" is a choice, not a weakness. A monthly flagship night (the Full Moon Series concept) plus a small number of regular nights avoids a frequency fight Rocket would win.
- **Watch:** Rocket could add photography or player features cheaply. Our edge has to come from production quality and a consistent look, not just from doing content at all.

## What Moon Padel should copy, adapt and do differently

Run Moon Padel on Rocket's operating backbone, then build the brand Rocket does not have. Anything with a fee, date, prize or venue stays a placeholder until the founders confirm it.

### Copy

1. **WhatsApp as the front door.** One keyword ("MOON"), one reply with next steps. Already our main CTA.
2. **Pay to register, no refunds, free transfer.** Online payment holds the spot; refunds become a transfer or credit up to [X] hours before. Protects cash flow and reduces no-shows.
3. **Free waitlist with a short claim window.** Fills dropouts without manual chasing.
4. **Fair levels from the first event.** Place players after their first night, and state the rule publicly.
5. **Clear public rules page.** Format, scoring, conduct, payment terms, in plain language.

### Adapt

1. **Levels with a story.** Use the moon-phase names (Crescent, Half moon, Gibbous, Full moon, still a concept) instead of D4 to D1, mapped to real level bands the founders set.
2. **Results that people share.** Same night: a results post, a winners photo, and medal gold used only for winners. Monthly: player of the month and pair of the month with a portrait, not a table row.
3. **A season, not just a race.** Rocket resets every month. Moon could run a season across [N] flagship nights with a final, so each night builds a storyline and rivalries.
4. **Start simple on tech.** A shared sheet and a results post are enough for the first events. Build an app only after demand is proven.

### Do differently

1. **Sell the night.** Lighting, music, a host on the mic, live scores, only if each element is real. Show it in every Reel.
2. **Every player leaves with content.** A clip or photo per player is the single biggest gap Rocket leaves open, and it turns players into promoters.
3. **Faces over spreadsheets.** Player features, pair stories, rivalries, behind the scenes. The website and grid should be full of real people at Moon nights.
4. **Venues as partners.** Offer each venue a Collab post, footage and new players, not just a court booking.
5. **Build sponsor and corporate offers early.** A sponsor-ready night format and a corporate night package open revenue Rocket does not show.
6. **Serve who Rocket ignores.** Test one category or night Rocket does not run, such as a women's night, mixed pairs or a newcomer night, once demand is checked.
7. **One consistent look.** Keep to the dark, cinematic brand system so every post is recognisable, where Rocket's look is functional and generic.
