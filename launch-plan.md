# Moon Padel 7-Day Instagram Launch Plan

Oct 30, 2026 · @Hola

## What the references tell us

All three reference videos are Rocket Padel ads shot at Central Padel Dubai, and they sell a system, not an experience. That is the gap Moon Padel should launch into.

**Working assumption:** the launch market is Dubai, because the references, venues and competitor are all Dubai. Confirm or correct before anything is posted.

| Ad | Length | Structure | Visual style |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### What the site shows

Facts from [rocketpadel.ae](https://rocketpadel.ae/), [rules](https://rocketpadel.ae/rules) and [schedule](https://rocketpadel.ae/schedule), checked today:

- Positioned as Dubai's competitive padel league: four divisions (D4 to D1), a 0 to 5 player rating, best 3 results count in a monthly race.
- Ladder format: 6 to 8 short rounds, winners move up a court, top-court wins weighted 1.5x.
- 170 AED per player, 90 to 120 minute events, 20 to 24 players each, mostly 5:20 PM to 8:50 PM starts.
- Sign-up through WhatsApp and a paid-spot booking page. Payments non-refundable, free transfer up to 24 hours before.
- Claims 1,452+ players and 244 tournaments. Clubs page lists Central Padel, Dock & Court, Padel Kingdom and WPA.
- Recognition is medals plus monthly Best Player and Best Pair per division. No sponsors or prizes shown.

### Observations (our read, not verified)

- **Strengths:** a clear, fair system; low-friction WhatsApp entry; standings and player profiles give people a reason to come back.
- **Weak spots:** the site has no photography and feels like a spreadsheet; ads all lean on the same "boring padel" problem; nothing about atmosphere, music, food, or the night itself.
- **Fill rates:** weekend events showed 0/20 to 7/20 spots taken a day or two out. This could mean late booking or soft demand. Worth watching, not concluding.

### What we take from this (without copying)

1. Level-matched play is now expected in Dubai. Moon Padel needs it, but should not lead with it. Rocket already owns that message.
2. WhatsApp sign-up works for this audience. Keep entry that simple.
3. Results and rankings drive return visits. Plan for a results format from the first event.

## Where Moon Padel wins

Rocket sells the league. Moon Padel sells the night. The launch should make people want to be there, then show that the competition is serious.

|  | Rocket Padel | Moon Padel (proposed) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core promise | Fair, level-matched games | A padel night that feels like an event |
| Hero of the content | The system (ratings, divisions, iPad) | The players and the atmosphere |
| Rhythm | Many events per week | Fewer, bigger nights with a signature format |
| Look | Bright courts, stock caption fonts, green accents | Dark, cinematic, lit like a stadium at night |
| What players take home | A rating change | A rating, plus their own highlight clip and photos |

### Three launch pillars

1. **After dark.** Moon Padel lives at night. Lighting, music, a host on the mic, live scores on screen. This is where the moon idea earns its place without any space graphics.
2. **Every player is the main character.** Each player leaves with a clip or photo of themselves. This is the strongest differentiator we have, and it turns every participant into a sharer.
3. **Real competition, fairly matched.** Levels, results and rankings, stated simply and confidently. Table stakes, not the headline.

### Concept lines (not approved, for founders to pick from)

- **"Every match, an event."** Matches the brief's core idea directly.
- **"Padel after dark."** Owns the night. Short enough for bios, posters and merch.
- **"Play under the lights."** Warmer and more inviting, less edgy.

### A bigger idea to consider: the Full Moon Series

A flagship Moon Padel night held once a month, close to the full moon. It gives the brand a natural monthly cadence, a built-in countdown, and a reason for the name that nobody else can claim. Smaller regular events can sit around it. Treat this as a concept until the founders decide the event model; exact full moon dates need checking against a lunar calendar.

## Before Day 1: setup and seeding

Teasers only work if someone sees them. A brand new account with no followers gets very little reach, so spend 3 to 5 days seeding before the first post.

### Grid order for the teasers

Instagram places the newest post top left. To make the first three posts form one composed row, post the **right** tile on Day 1, the **middle** on Day 2, the **left** on Day 3. Design all three together, cropped to 3:4 grid tiles.

## Days 1 to 3: the three teasers

The teasers show night, light and sound, and never say what Moon Padel is. Each Reel gets a custom cover from the design canvas, and the covers run as a moon-phase trilogy: the founders' moon mark as a crescent (Day 1), a half moon (Day 2), then a full moon over the lit court (Day 3). A shared net line runs across all three, so the finished grid row reads as one piece.

### Day 1: "Something rises" (right tile)

**Job:** stop the scroll and make the name feel like something.

**Concept:** a padel ball in total darkness, lit from one side so it looks like a crescent moon. No court, no players. Just the ball becoming a moon.

**Hook (first second):** black screen, then a thin silver edge of light appears. The eye has to work out what it is.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1s | Black, faint felt texture just visible | none |
| 1 to 4s | Side light slowly sweeps in, crescent of the ball grows | none |
| 4 to 6s | Macro push-in, felt fibres and seam sharp, crescent at its fullest | "Something rises after dark." |
| 6 to 8s | Hard cut to black, loops cleanly back to the start | none |

**Sound:** a low hum, then one deep ball bounce on the cut to black.

**Edit:** slow, minimal, no transitions. Cool silver grade, deep blacks. Text small, centred, lots of space.

**Caption:** Not yet.

**Stories:** reshare the Reel. Then a poll: "When do you play your best padel?" Before work / After dark.

### Day 2: "Listen" (middle tile)

**Job:** build intrigue and get the first shares.

**Concept:** a real rally played in darkness. Every time a racket hits the ball, a flash of light reveals a split second of the court: a racket, the glass, a player's eyes, the net. Between hits, black.

**Hook:** "Sound on." on black, then the first hit cracks.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1s | Black | "Sound on." |
| 1 to 7s | 6 to 8 hits, each a flash of 3 to 5 frames, rhythm speeding up | none |
| 7 to 9s | A smash, longest flash, ball into the glass | none |
| 9 to 10s | Black, ball rolling sound fades | "Some nights are louder." |

**How to make it:** film a normal rally under court lights at 60fps. In the edit, black out every frame except the few around each hit. No strobe or special lighting needed.

**Sound:** real court audio only, boosted. No music. The rhythm of the rally is the track.

**Caption:** Sound on. Then send this to the person you always play with.

**Stories:** reshare, then a question sticker: "Guess what's coming." Reply to the best guesses publicly the next day.

### Day 3: "Lights on" (left tile, completes the row)

**Job:** reveal the date and set up Day 4.

**Concept:** an empty court at night. The floodlight banks switch on one at a time with a heavy clunk. The camera rises over the glass as the last bank lights. The moon mark appears for one second, unexplained, then the reveal day and time.

**Hook:** total darkness, then the first clunk and a single light bank.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1s | Dark court, silhouette of the glass | none |
| 1 to 6s | Light banks switch on one by one, gimbal rising behind the glass | none |
| 6 to 8s | Court fully lit, wide and still | none |
| 8 to 9s | Cut to black, moon mark fades in | none |
| 9 to 12s | Mark holds | "\[Reveal day\]. 8 PM." |

**Sound:** one clunk per light bank, then a rising tone that cuts out on the mark.

**Caption:** Lights on. \[Reveal day\], 8 PM.

**Stories:** reshare, then a countdown sticker to the reveal so followers get a reminder. Swap the profile picture to the moon mark today if it is not already.

## Days 4 to 7: reveal, explain, engage, convert

### Day 4: the reveal

**Job:** say clearly what Moon Padel is and open the founding players list.

**Formats:** a 25 to 30s brand film Reel as the main post, posted as a Collab with the venue partner and 3 to 5 players. The racket-shadow poster from the canvas goes up in Stories and can lead the Reel as its cover.

**Hook:** the full moon ball from Day 3 drops into frame and is smashed. Hard cut to a lit court full of players.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 2s | Ball-moon falls, smash, cut | none |
| 2 to 6s | Players arriving at night, bags down, lights on | "When the city slows down," |
| 6 to 10s | Rallies in slow motion, glass, net | "the courts light up." |
| 10 to 15s | Host on the mic, live score on screen, crowd reacting | "Real matches. Matched levels." |
| 15 to 20s | Camera filming players, a player watching their own clip on a phone | "Every player, on camera." |
| 20 to 26s | Celebration, group photo, logo resolves | "MoonPadel. Play beyond." |
| 26 to 30s | End card with CTA | "Founding players list open. WhatsApp MOON." |

Only film the host, screen and camera crew if those will genuinely be part of the events. If not, cut those shots and lines.

**Caption:**

This is MoonPadel. Play beyond.

Competitive padel nights in \[city\], built on real matches, fair levels and an atmosphere worth showing up for.

Our founding players list is open now. Get on it and you hear about the first Moon Night before anyone else.

WhatsApp "MOON" through the link in our bio.

\#padeldubai #dubaipadel #padel

**Stories:** the reveal poster with a link sticker to WhatsApp; a poll on which category people would enter (Men's / Women's / Mixed); swap the bio to the full version and unhide the highlights.

**Caption:** Everything you need to know before the first Moon Night. Save this one, and send it to whoever you are bringing.

**Stories:** reshare slide 2 with a question sticker: "What would make a padel night worth your Friday?" The answers feed the founders' Reel on Day 6.

### Day 6: which phase are you? (Stories only)

**Job:** get people talking about their level, collect useful data for the first event, and keep the grid clean. There is no feed post today, so the moon-phase teaser row stays a complete row.

**Concept:** Moon Padel's own level names, drawn as moon phases. A clear contrast to Rocket's D4 to D1. The names are a concept; the real level bands still need to be set by the founders.

| Phase | Who it describes |
| --- | --- |
| Crescent | Getting into competing |
| Half moon | Solid club player |
| Gibbous | Strong and competitive |
| Full moon | Top level in the city |

**Story sequence:**

1. The Which phase are you? design with a quiz sticker.
2. A slider sticker: "How competitive do you want your padel nights?"
3. A question sticker: "Who would you bring to a Moon Night?"
4. The founding players list story with the WhatsApp link.

**Next day:** share the quiz split as a Story (for example, "Most of you are half moons") and use it to size categories for the first event.

### Day 7: the first Moon Night

**Job:** turn interest into names on a list, or registrations if details are ready.

**Format:** the Full Moon Series poster on the canvas as a single post or carousel cover. Pick a version based on what is confirmed.

**Version A, details confirmed:** date, time, venue, categories and fee on the poster and carousel slides. Registration opens via WhatsApp "MOON". Mention a spot limit only if there is a real cap.

**Version B, details not confirmed:** headline the first Moon Night with the month only, and keep the CTA on the founding players list. Founding players get the details first, which gives the list real value.

**Caption, Version A:** The first Moon Night. \[Date\], \[time\], \[venue\]. \[Categories\]. \[Fee\] per player. WhatsApp "MOON" to register.

**Caption, Version B:** The first Moon Night is coming in \[month\]. Founding players get the date, venue and first access before we announce it here. WhatsApp "MOON" to get on the list.

**Stories:** the founding list template with a link sticker; a countdown sticker to registration opening (Version B) or to event day (Version A).

## One-night shoot plan

Everything for the week can be filmed in one 3 to 4 hour evening at a single venue. Book a court from about 8 PM so the outside is fully dark, and get the venue's written OK to film.

**Crew and kit:** 1 videographer with gimbal, 1 phone or second camera for vertical BTS, a small side light (LED panel) for the ball macro, a tripod. Shoot vertical 4K, 60fps for anything in slow motion.

**People:** 8 to 12 players across levels, dressed in navy, black or white with no big rival logos.

- [ ] Ball macro: padel ball on black, side light sweeping to make the crescent (Day 1)
- [ ] A full rally under court lights, close and wide, clean audio (Day 2)
- [ ] Empty court, light banks switching off then on one at a time, gimbal rise from behind the glass (Day 3)
- [ ] Ball dropping into frame and a smash, slow motion (Day 4 hook)
- [ ] Arrivals: car park to court, bags down, shoes on, fist bumps (Day 4)
- [ ] Rallies in slow motion: lobs off the glass, smashes, dives (Day 4, future Reels)
- [ ] Reactions: celebrations, high fives, laughter between points (Day 4)
- [ ] Host or score screen, only if part of the real event (Day 4)
- [ ] A player watching their own clip on a phone (Day 4, Day 5)
- [ ] Group photo on court, lights behind (Day 4 end card)
- [ ] Close-ups of players at different levels, for future phase-themed content
- [ ] A ball held up to the court lights, for Story backgrounds (optional)
- [ ] 20 to 30 still photos of players for future player spotlights

Anything left over goes into a footage bank for the weeks after launch, so the account never goes quiet between events.

## KPIs and what we need from the founders

The launch week succeeds if it puts real padel players on the founding list, not if it gains followers. There is no baseline yet, so this week sets one.

| Metric | Where to read it | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp "MOON" messages | WhatsApp, count daily | The main conversion this week |
| Profile visits and link taps | Instagram insights | Shows the teasers and reveal created intent |
| Shares and saves per post | Instagram insights | Shares spread the teasers, saves show the carousel was useful |
| 3-second hold and average watch time on Reels | Reel insights | Tells us which hooks to reuse |
| Followers from \[city\] | Audience insights | Checks we are reaching the right market |
| Story poll and question replies | Stories | Free research for the first event |

Review every evening. If a teaser hook holds attention well, reuse that style in week 2.
