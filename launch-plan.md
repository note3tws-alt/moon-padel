# Moon Padel Dubai: 7-Day Instagram Launch Plan

Updated 15 Nov 2026 for the Dubai launch, using the [Rocket Padel competitor study](research/rocket-padel-competitor-study.md). First version 30 Oct 2026 · @Hola

## What changed in this version

- **Dubai is the launch market.** All `[city]` placeholders are now Dubai. Fees are written as AED `[FEE]`.
- **Competitive first, then atmosphere.** Rocket's ads attack "social games" and "pizza nights". If Moon Padel looks like a party, that message lands against us. Every post now shows levels, live scores and same-night results alongside the night itself.
- **A rhythm on purpose.** Fewer, bigger nights: one monthly flagship (the Full Moon Series concept) plus `[N]` regular nights, building to a season final. We don't try to match Rocket's 11 games a week.
- **Rocket's backbone, copied.** WhatsApp entry, pay to register, no refunds but free transfer, a free waitlist, fair levels from night one, and a public rules page.
- **Partners from day one.** Venues get a filmed, branded night and a Collab post, not just a court booking. Sponsor and corporate offers get drafted before launch.
- **Dubai timing.** Posting and event times follow Dubai evenings, the Saturday and Sunday weekend, and the cooler season.

### Three launch pillars

1. **Real competition, fairly matched.** Levels set after your first night, live scores, results posted the same night. This now sits in every post, so nobody mistakes us for a social mixer.
2. **After dark.** Dubai plays at night. Lighting, music, a host on the mic and a score screen, each one only if it is real at our events.
3. **Every player is the main character.** Each player leaves with a clip or photo of themselves. Rocket leaves this completely open, and it turns every player into a promoter.

### Concept lines (not approved, for founders to pick from)

- **"Every match, an event."** Matches the brief's core idea.
- **"Padel after dark."** Owns the night. Short enough for bios, posters and merch.
- **"Dubai's padel nights."** Plain and local. Good for search and the bio.

"Play beyond." from the founders' deck stays as the brand line.

### Event rhythm: the Full Moon Series (concept)

- **Flagship:** one Moon Night a month, close to the full moon. Exact dates must be checked against a lunar calendar and venue availability.
- **Regular nights:** `[N]` smaller nights between flagships, if demand supports them.
- **Season:** results across `[N]` flagship nights feed a season final, so each night builds a storyline and rivalries. Monthly player and pair of the month are announced with a portrait, not a table row.
- **Dubai calendar:** the cooler months (roughly November to March) suit outdoor courts. Ramadan 2027 is expected around February to March: late evening play after iftar is common, so plan a schedule for it once the dates are confirmed. Summer nights may need indoor or covered courts.

## How entry works (copied from what works in Dubai)

1. **WhatsApp "MOON".** One keyword, one reply with the next steps. The link in the bio is a click-to-chat link with "MOON" prefilled.
2. **Pay to register.** Paying holds the spot. Payment provider `[TO CONFIRM]`.
3. **No refunds, free transfer.** A paid spot can move to another night or to a friend at the same level up to `[X]` hours before.
4. **Free waitlist.** When a spot opens, the first person on the list gets a short window to take it.
5. **Levels.** New players are placed after their first night, using level bands the founders set. The rule is written on the rules page.
6. **Rules page.** Format, scoring, conduct and payment terms in plain language, linked from the bio and the WhatsApp reply.

A shared sheet and a results post are enough for the first nights. An app can wait until demand is proven.

## Days 1 to 3: the three teasers

The teasers show night, light and sound, and never say what Moon Padel is. Each Reel gets a custom cover, running as a moon-phase trilogy: crescent (Day 1), half moon (Day 2), full moon over the lit court (Day 3). The Day 1 cover now carries "DUBAI · AFTER DARK" so local players know it is for them.

### Day 1: "Something rises" (right tile)

**Job:** stop the scroll and make the name feel like something in Dubai.

**Concept:** a padel ball in total darkness, lit from one side so it looks like a crescent moon.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1s | Black, faint felt texture just visible | none |
| 1 to 4s | Side light slowly sweeps in, crescent of the ball grows | none |
| 4 to 6s | Macro push-in, felt fibres and seam sharp | "Something rises after dark." |
| 6 to 8s | Hard cut to black, loops back to the start | "Dubai." |

**Sound:** a low hum, then one deep ball bounce on the cut to black.

**Edit:** slow, minimal, no transitions. Cool silver grade, deep blacks.

**Caption:** Not yet. Dubai.

**Stories:** reshare. Poll: "When do you play your best padel?" Before work / After dark.

### Day 2: "Sound on" (middle tile)

**Job:** build intrigue, and make it clear this is serious padel, not a social game.

**Concept:** a real, fast rally at a high level, played in darkness. Each racket hit flashes a split second of the court. Between hits, black.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1s | Black | "Sound on." |
| 1 to 7s | 6 to 8 hits, each a flash of 3 to 5 frames, rhythm speeding up | none |
| 7 to 9s | A smash, longest flash, ball into the glass | none |
| 9 to 10s | Black, ball rolling sound fades | "Some nights are louder." |

**How to make it:** film a genuinely competitive rally under court lights at 60fps, then black out every frame except the few around each hit. Use strong players for this one.

**Sound:** real court audio only, boosted. No music.

**Caption:** Sound on. Send this to the partner who never lets a ball go.

**Stories:** reshare, then a question sticker: "Guess what's coming to Dubai." Reply to the best guesses the next day.

### Day 3: "Lights on" (left tile, completes the row)

**Job:** reveal the date and set up Day 4.

**Concept:** an empty court at night. Floodlight banks switch on one at a time. The camera rises over the glass, the moon mark appears for one second, then the reveal day and time.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 to 1s | Dark court, silhouette of the glass | none |
| 1 to 6s | Light banks switch on one by one, gimbal rising | none |
| 6 to 8s | Court fully lit, wide and still | none |
| 8 to 9s | Cut to black, moon mark fades in | none |
| 9 to 12s | Mark holds | "\[Reveal day\]. 8 PM." |

**Caption:** Lights on. \[Reveal day\], 8 PM.

**Stories:** reshare, then a countdown sticker to the reveal.

**Job:** turn interest into names on the list, or registrations if the details are ready.

**Stories:** the founding list template with a link sticker; a countdown sticker to registration opening (Version B) or to event day (Version A).

## One-night shoot plan

Everything for the week can be filmed in one 3 to 4 hour evening at a single Dubai venue. Book from about 8 PM so it is fully dark, and get the venue's written OK to film.

**Crew and kit:** 1 videographer with gimbal, 1 phone or second camera for vertical BTS, a small LED panel for the ball macro, a tripod. Shoot vertical 4K, 60fps for slow motion.

**People:** 8 to 12 players across levels, with at least 4 strong players for the competitive rallies. Navy, black or white kit, no big rival logos.

- [ ] Ball macro on black, side light sweeping into a crescent (Day 1)
- [ ] A fast, high-level rally under court lights, close and wide, clean audio (Day 2)
- [ ] Empty court, light banks switching on one at a time, gimbal rise (Day 3)
- [ ] Ball dropping into frame and a smash, slow motion (Day 4 hook)
- [ ] Arrivals: car park to court, bags down, fist bumps (Day 4)
- [ ] Competitive points: lobs off the glass, smashes, long rallies (Day 4, future Reels)
- [ ] A score screen or scoresheet being updated, and results being posted (Day 4, Day 5)
- [ ] Winners with medals (Day 4 end, results templates)
- [ ] A player watching their own clip on a phone (Day 4, Day 5)
- [ ] Group photo on court, lights behind (Day 4 end card)
- [ ] Close-ups of players at different levels, for phase-themed content
- [ ] 20 to 30 stills of players for future player of the month and pair features

Leftover footage goes into a bank for the weeks after launch.

## KPIs

Launch week succeeds if it puts real Dubai padel players on the founding list, not if it gains followers. There is no baseline yet, so this week sets one.

| Metric | Where to read it | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp "MOON" messages | WhatsApp, count daily | The main conversion this week |
| Profile visits and link taps | Instagram insights | Shows the teasers and reveal created intent |
| Shares and saves per post | Instagram insights | Shares spread the teasers, saves show the carousel was useful |
| 3-second hold and average watch time | Reel insights | Tells us which hooks to reuse |
| Followers in Dubai | Audience insights | Checks we are reaching the right market |
| Phase quiz and night poll replies | Stories | Sizes categories and picks the first event night |
| Venue and sponsor conversations started | Founders' log | Early sign of partner revenue |

Review every evening. If a teaser hook holds attention well, reuse that style in week 2.
