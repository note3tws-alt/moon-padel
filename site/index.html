The format

The MoonPadel
Ladder Americano.

Our own format, built for close matches and a big finish.

16Players
4Courts
6Rounds
15Minutes a round
Court 1Both winning pairs from courts 1 and 2
Court 2Losers from court 1 + winners from court 3
Court 3Losers from court 2 + winners from court 4
Court 4Both losing pairs from courts 3 and 4

Pairs split on every new court, so you always get a new partner. Full rules →

How it works

From message
to podium

Send “MOON”

Message us on WhatsApp. We chat about your game, give you a level tag and send you the next nights for your level.

Check in at the tablet

Tap your name on the MoonPadel tablet at the desk. The TV shows your court and your first partner.

Climb the ladder

Six timed rounds. Win and your pair moves up a court, lose and you move down. New partner every round.

Podium and medals

Every game you win counts. Top three take gold, silver and bronze, and your points add to a lifetime total.

Why Moon Padel

Every match,
an event.

Players are placed by level after their first night, so every match is close and every result counts. This is competitive padel, not a social mixer.

03

Players, not rows
