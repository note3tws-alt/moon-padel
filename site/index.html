Levels

Which phase
are you?

Four levels, named after the moon. You start where you fit and move up as you win.

Crescent

Getting into competing. You can hold a rally and want proper matches against people at your stage.

Half moon

Solid all round. You know your side, you play regularly and you want tighter, faster matches.

Gibbous

Strong and competitive. Good tactics, reliable at the net and used to playing tournaments.

Full moon

The top level in Dubai amateur padel. Fast, precise and out to win every point.
