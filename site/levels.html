How placement works

Start where you fit. Move as you win.

  1. Before your first night, tell us how you play when you WhatsApp “MOON”. We suggest a starting level.
  2. After your first night, we confirm your level from your results.
  3. After every night, levels are reviewed. Win consistently and you move up. [PROMOTION AND RELEGATION RULE]

Play at your real level. Sitting below it to collect wins ruins the night for everyone, and players who do it are moved up.
