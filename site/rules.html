Rules

How a Ladder
night works

The format, the points and the entry terms, in plain language. Every player agrees to these when they register.

LevelsFormatPoints & medals RegistrationTransfersWaitlist On the nightFair playFilming

1. Levels

  • Our team gives every player a level tag, for example D-, D or D+ (C-, C and C+ above them). You do not pick it yourself.
  • Each night is set up for a level group, for example D-, D and D+. The night lists which tags it includes.
  • The strongest tags start on court 1 and the least experienced on the last court.
  • Tags are reviewed by the team between nights. [PROMOTION AND RELEGATION RULE]

2. Format: the MoonPadel Ladder Americano

  • 16 players, 4 courts, 6 rounds of about 15 minutes each. You sign up on your own.
  • Partners change every round. After each round the winning pair moves up one court and the losing pair moves down one. Winners on court 1 and losers on court 4 stay where they are.
  • On your new court the pairs split, so you play with a new partner and against new opponents. The TV shows your court and partner before every round.
  • Each round is timed. When the whistle goes, the score is the number of games each team won in that round, for example 4-2.
  • One team enters the score on the tablet at the desk and the other team confirms it. Only a confirmed score counts. If you disagree, tap “That is wrong” and the organiser settles it.
  • Level score at the whistle: [TIE RULE: GOLDEN POINT OR ORGANISER PICKS WHO MOVES UP].

3. Points, podium and medals

  • Every game your team wins is yours. Win 4-2 and you and your partner get 4 points each; your opponents get 2 each.
  • After 6 rounds the three highest totals take gold, silver and bronze for the night.
  • Level on points? Most total games first, then most round wins, then best game difference, then the organiser decides under the published rule.
  • Your points also go on a lifetime total that never resets. Reach a milestone and you unlock its medal, once: [MILESTONE POINTS].
  • Results, the podium and your lifetime total show on your player profile the same night.

4. Registration and payment

  • WhatsApp “MOON” to get registration details for upcoming nights.
  • Paying registers you. Your spot is not held until payment goes through.
  • Entry is AED [FEE] per player, shown on each night. Payment by [PAYMENT PROVIDER]. [STATE WHETHER VAT AND FEES ARE INCLUDED]
  • Registration closes [X] hours before the night, or when it is full.

5. Refunds and transfers

  • Entry fees are not refunded.
  • You can move your paid spot to another night at your level, or give it to another player at your level, for free up to [X] hours before the start. Message us on WhatsApp to do it.
  • If Moon Padel cancels a night, you can move your spot to another night or get [CREDIT OR REFUND].

6. Waitlist

  • When a night is full you can join the waitlist for free.
  • If a spot opens, the first person on the list is offered it and has [X] minutes to pay. If they do not, it goes to the next person.

7. On the night

  • Check in on the MoonPadel tablet at the desk by tapping your name, [X] minutes before the start. Play cannot start until all 16 players are in, so a late arrival may be replaced from the reserve list.
  • Bring your racket and court shoes. Balls: [PROVIDED OR BRING YOUR OWN].
  • Follow the venue’s rules. Moon Padel runs the event; the venue runs the facility.

8. Fair play and conduct

  • Call your own lines honestly. If you are not sure, the point goes to your opponents.
  • Play at your real level. Players who stay below their level to win are moved up, and repeat cases can be removed from future nights.
  • Respect opponents, partners, venue staff and the Moon Padel team. Abuse of any kind means you leave the night without a refund.

9. Filming and photos

  • Moon Nights are filmed and photographed for Moon Padel’s channels.
  • If you would rather not be featured, tell the team at check-in and we will leave you out of posts.

10. Changes

We may update these rules between nights. The version on this page applies to the next night you register for. Questions? Message us on WhatsApp.
