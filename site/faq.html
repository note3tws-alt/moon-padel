FAQ

Questions

Quick answers. For anything else, WhatsApp us.

What is Moon Padel?

An independent padel tournament and events brand in Dubai. We run competitive padel nights at partner venues. We are not a club and we do not run courts.

How do I sign up?

WhatsApp “MOON”. You join the founding players list and we send you upcoming nights and how to register. Paying holds your spot.

What level do I need to be?

Any competitive level. There are four levels, from Crescent to Full moon, and you only play people at yours. See Levels.

Do I need a partner?

Each night says whether you sign up on your own, as a pair, or either. [CONFIRM FORMAT]

How much does it cost?

Entry is AED [FEE] per player. The price is shown on every night before you pay.

What if I can’t make it?

Fees are not refunded, but you can move your spot to another night or give it to a player at your level for free up to [X] hours before.

The night is full. Can I still play?

Join the free waitlist. If a spot opens, the first person in line gets [X] minutes to take it.

Where and when are the nights?

At partner venues in Dubai, in the evening. Every night lists its venue, date and start time on Events.

Is this a social game?

No. It is competitive padel with levels, live scores and same-night results. The night around it is what makes it feel like an event.

Will I be filmed?

Nights are filmed and photographed. If you would rather not appear in posts, tell the team at check-in.

Will there be women’s, mixed or newcomer nights?

We want to add formats people ask for. Tell us on WhatsApp what you would play and we will plan around demand.

Can my company book a private night?

Yes. See corporate nights or WhatsApp “PARTNER”.
